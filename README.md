# Tracewire

Tracewire is a free Android app that shows, live, which app on your phone is talking to which server, who owns it, where in the world it is and how much data it uses. It blocks ads and trackers, has a firewall and encrypted DNS, and keeps everything on your phone. No adverts, no account, no root.
