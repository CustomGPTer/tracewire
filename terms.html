Using Tracewire within the law

Tracewire shows what a phone's apps connect to, and can record that traffic. Use it on phones you own, or with the clear agreement of the person who uses the phone.

  • Investigatory Powers Act 2016. Intercepting someone else's communications without lawful authority can be a crime.
  • Computer Misuse Act 1990. Installing or using software on a phone you are not allowed to use is an offence.
  • UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Records of another person's internet use are their personal data. Keeping or sharing them brings legal duties.
  • Protection from Harassment Act 1997. Using any app to watch or follow someone can be harassment.
  • At work. Monitoring staff phones means meeting an employer's duties under data protection law, including telling them.

Laws differ in other countries. If you are not in the UK, the rules where you are apply, and you are responsible for following them. Before downloading from this site you are asked to confirm: “I understand and will use this app within the law”.
