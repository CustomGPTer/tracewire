Release notes

What changed in each version of Tracewire. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 24 November 2026Latest

  • First release. See, live, which app on your phone is talking to which server, who owns it, where it is and how much data it uses.
  • Live connections with the server's name, address, owner, country and data each way, searchable, with filters for open, blocked, trackers, web, look-ups and failed.
  • The world map, each app's privacy grade, its sites and companies, activity today and the week.
  • Protection: ad and tracker blocker with per-app exceptions, a firewall for every app (Wi-Fi, mobile, roaming, screen off, not on screen), blocked sites and countries, Lockdown, and new apps held until you allow them.
  • Encrypted DNS through Quad9, Cloudflare or Mullvad, with automatic fallback.
  • Data plan with forecast and daily budget, Android's own usage records, alerts, and the overnight report.
  • Reports and exports: PDF privacy report, CSV connection list and PCAP packet capture.
  • Two home screen widgets, a Quick Settings tile, an optional fingerprint or PIN lock, and light and dark looks.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called Tracewire.apk. It is 9.2 MB (9,216,778 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

f96c9108419d648e06d5116b2ba9c2a5d13e41d09d0d04202ee2269e2c94c0ef

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Tracewire.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps your history, rules and settings.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
